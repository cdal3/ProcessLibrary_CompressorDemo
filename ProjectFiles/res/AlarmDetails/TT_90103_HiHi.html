Alarm Response Plan

Tag: TT-90103 — Compressor Oil Temperature HiHi

1. Alarm Summary

Alarm TagTT-90103.HiHi
MeasurementCompressor lube oil temperature
Alarm DescriptionOil temperature has exceeded the HiHi trip setpoint — motor trip via interlock
Trip Setpoint (Example)> 85 °C
PriorityCRITICAL
Alarm ClassSafety / Equipment Protection
Response TimeImmediate (< 2 minutes)
Consequence of InactionBearing damage, oil degradation, seizure of rotating equipment

2. Possible Causes

  • Loss or reduction of cooling water flow to the oil cooler.
  • High cooling water supply temperature.
  • Fouled oil cooler (tube-side or shell-side).
  • Low lube oil flow (pump problem, filter plugging, low reservoir level).
  • Degraded oil (viscosity loss, contamination).
  • Bearing distress generating additional heat.
  • PID (TIC-90103) not controlling cooling water valve correctly.
Safety Notice: This alarm should have already tripped MT-90100 via the interlock. Verify the motor has stopped. If it has not, treat as a loss-of-safety event and initiate a manual trip.

3. Operator Response Actions

  1. Acknowledge the alarm and confirm MT-90100 has tripped (raP_Opr_Seq Seq state = Stopped).
  2. Verify auxiliary lube oil pump has auto-started (if configured) to continue oil circulation for post-lube.
  3. Isolate downstream users or transfer to spare unit.
  4. Notify Shift Supervisor and Maintenance.
  5. Do not attempt restart until oil temperature is < 60 °C AND root cause is confirmed.

4. Troubleshooting Guidance

  1. Check cooling water supply pressure, temperature, and valve position (TCV-90103).
  2. Inspect oil cooler for fouling — compare inlet/outlet temperatures for approach.
  3. Check oil pump discharge pressure and reservoir level.
  4. Sample oil for particulate, water content, and viscosity.
  5. Review TIC-90103 controller behavior — see TIC_90103_HiDev plan.
  6. Compare TT-90103 with adjacent RTDs / bearing temps for sensor sanity.

5. Reset and Restart Criteria

  • Oil temperature stable < 60 °C with cooling loop restored.
  • Root cause corrected and documented.
  • Oil sample results acceptable if oil condition was suspected.
  • Shift Supervisor approval.
  • Reset HiHi interlock at MT-90100 faceplate, then follow standard startup.
