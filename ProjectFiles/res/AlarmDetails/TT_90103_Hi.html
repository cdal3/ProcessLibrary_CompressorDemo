Alarm Response Plan

Tag: TT-90103 — Compressor Oil Temperature Hi

1. Alarm Summary

Alarm TagTT-90103.Hi
MeasurementCompressor lube oil temperature
Alarm DescriptionOil temperature has exceeded the Hi warning setpoint — pre-alarm before HiHi trip
Hi Setpoint (Example)> 75 °C
PriorityMEDIUM
Alarm ClassProcess / Early Warning
Response Time< 15 minutes
Consequence of InactionEscalation to HiHi trip, motor shutdown, potential bearing damage

2. Possible Causes

  • Rising cooling water supply temperature (seasonal, cooling tower issue).
  • Partial fouling of oil cooler.
  • Reduced cooling water flow (partially closed valve, plugged strainer).
  • Increased process load raising oil heat rejection.
  • TIC-90103 controller sluggish or de-tuned.
  • Oil degradation lowering heat-transfer performance.
Note: This is a pre-alarm intended to give the operator time to intervene before the HiHi trip. Prompt corrective action typically avoids a compressor trip.

3. Operator Response Actions

  1. Acknowledge the alarm and review the last 30 minutes of TT-90103 and TIC-90103 trends.
  2. Verify TIC-90103 is in AUTO and the cooling water valve is opening as expected.
  3. Check cooling water supply temperature, pressure, and strainer differential pressure.
  4. If TIC-90103 output is at 100% and temperature is still rising, prepare for a possible manual load reduction on the compressor.
  5. If temperature approaches HiHi (within 5 °C), notify Shift Supervisor and consider proactive load reduction or planned shutdown.

4. Troubleshooting Guidance

  1. Compare cooling water supply/return ΔT against baseline.
  2. Check for recent changes in ambient conditions or cooling tower performance.
  3. Inspect the oil cooler outlet temperature vs. water inlet temperature (approach).
  4. Review oil condition history (last sample, hours since change).
  5. Verify TT-90103 reading against a redundant bearing RTD if available.

5. Return to Normal

  • Oil temperature returns below the Hi setpoint minus deadband.
  • Root cause corrected (cooling restored, strainer cleaned, controller re-tuned, etc.).
  • Alarm clears automatically once temperature normalizes.
