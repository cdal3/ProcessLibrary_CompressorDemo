Alarm Response Plan

Tag: TT-90103 — Compressor Oil Temperature Failure

1. Alarm Summary

Alarm TagTT-90103.Fail
MeasurementCompressor lube oil temperature (RTD / transmitter)
Alarm DescriptionSignal outside valid range or bad quality — measurement is unreliable
PriorityHIGH
Alarm ClassInstrument Integrity
Response TimeImmediate (< 5 minutes)
Consequence of InactionLoss of oil temperature protection — possible spurious trip or, worse, no protection against real high oil temperature

2. Possible Causes

  • Open or shorted RTD / thermocouple element.
  • Broken or loose field wiring.
  • Failed transmitter or power supply.
  • IO module channel fault.
  • Signal below 4 mA or above 20 mA (out of range).
  • Recent maintenance activity left instrument in an incorrect state.
Safety Notice: The oil temperature HiHi interlock relies on TT-90103. Follow the site procedure for operating with a failed safety-related instrument — this may require MOC approval, a temporary compensating measure (increased rounds, manual gauge readings), or a controlled shutdown.

3. Operator Response Actions

  1. Acknowledge the alarm and record the time.
  2. Check TT-90103 raw value and status on the faceplate (bad quality, out of range, over/under range).
  3. Cross-check with any redundant temperature indication (bearing RTDs, local gauge).
  4. Notify Shift Supervisor. Determine whether the interlock is configured to trip on bad quality (fail-safe) or hold last value.
  5. If the interlock is bypassed by the failure, place a bypass tag per site MOC and increase surveillance.
  6. Dispatch Instrument Technician.

4. Troubleshooting Guidance

  1. At the field cabinet, measure loop current (should be 4 - 20 mA).
  2. Verify transmitter power (24 VDC) and configuration.
  3. For RTD input, measure resistance at the module terminals; compare against expected value for current temperature.
  4. Check wiring for opens, shorts, or ground faults.
  5. Verify IO channel scaling and configuration on the PLC.
  6. Replace transmitter or RTD if bench test confirms failure.
Note: After replacement, perform a two-point calibration and verify the reading in the control system matches a certified test instrument before removing any bypass.

5. Return to Normal

  • Signal restored to Good quality and reading matches local reference within tolerance.
  • Any bypass on the HiHi interlock removed and MOC closed.
  • Alarm cleared at the faceplate.
