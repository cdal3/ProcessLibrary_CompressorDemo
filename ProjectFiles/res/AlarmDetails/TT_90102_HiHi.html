Alarm Response Plan

Tag: TT-90102 — Compressor Outlet Air Temperature HiHi

1. Alarm Summary

Alarm TagTT-90102.HiHi
MeasurementCompressor discharge (outlet) air temperature
Alarm DescriptionDischarge air temperature exceeded the HiHi trip setpoint — motor trip via interlock
Trip Setpoint (Example)> 120 °C
PriorityCRITICAL
Alarm ClassSafety / Equipment Protection
Response TimeImmediate (< 2 minutes)
Consequence of InactionAftercooler damage, downstream desiccant/filter damage, potential oil carryover ignition risk

2. Possible Causes

  • Loss or reduction of intercooler / aftercooler cooling water.
  • High cooling water supply temperature.
  • Fouled aftercooler tubes / plates.
  • High suction temperature or high suction pressure creating high discharge temp.
  • Excessive compression ratio (high back-pressure downstream).
  • Internal valve failure or worn compression element.
  • Faulty TT-90102 element reading high.
Safety Notice: High compressor discharge temperature is a fire-risk condition on lubricated compressors due to oil carryover. Verify MT-90100 has tripped and isolate the discharge line.

3. Operator Response Actions

  1. Acknowledge the alarm and confirm MT-90100 has tripped via the interlock.
  2. Verify aftercooler cooling water flow is maintained during coast-down.
  3. Isolate the discharge to downstream users; vent to atmosphere per SOP if required.
  4. Notify Shift Supervisor and Maintenance immediately.
  5. Do not restart until discharge temperature has cooled below 60 °C and root cause is confirmed.

4. Troubleshooting Guidance

  1. Check cooling water supply pressure/temperature and aftercooler valve position.
  2. Inspect aftercooler for fouling / bypass leakage.
  3. Review compressor suction temperature, pressure, and discharge pressure trend leading up to the trip.
  4. Check for downstream valve closures that raised discharge pressure.
  5. Sample oil and inspect for carryover / discoloration.
  6. Verify TT-90102 against a redundant sensor if available.

5. Reset and Restart Criteria

  • Discharge temperature stable and cooled per procedure.
  • Aftercooler condition verified.
  • Root cause corrected and documented.
  • Shift Supervisor approval logged.
  • Reset HiHi interlock at MT-90100 faceplate, then follow standard startup.
