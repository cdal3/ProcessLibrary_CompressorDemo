Alarm Response Plan

Tag: MT-90100 — Compressor Motor Drive Faulted

1. Alarm Summary

Alarm TagMT-90100.DriveFault
EquipmentCompressor Motor MT-90100 VFD / Soft-Starter
Alarm DescriptionDrive has declared a fault condition and stopped the motor
PriorityHIGH
Alarm ClassEquipment Protection
Response TimeImmediate (< 5 minutes)
Consequence of InactionLoss of process availability, potential drive damage on repeated reset attempts

2. Possible Causes

Drive Fault CategoryTypical Codes (Example)Likely Cause
Overcurrent / Ground FaultF5, F13Motor short, cable insulation failure, sudden mechanical load
Overvoltage / UndervoltageF6, F7Utility disturbance, regen from decel, DC bus issue
Drive OvertemperatureF8Blocked cooling fan, high ambient, dirty filter

Safety Notice: Do NOT repeatedly reset a drive fault without identifying the cause. Repeated resets on an overcurrent or ground fault can cause severe drive or motor damage.

3. Operator Response Actions

  1. Acknowledge the alarm and note the time.
  2. Open the MT-90100 drive faceplate — record the exact fault code and description.
  3. Verify the motor has stopped and secure downstream users.
  4. Notify the Shift Supervisor and Electrical Technician.
  5. Do not reset the drive until the cause is identified and site policy allows.

4. Troubleshooting Guidance

  1. Retrieve the drive fault log and event stack (last 5 faults).
  2. For overcurrent / ground fault: megger the motor before any reset attempt.
  3. For overtemperature: inspect drive cooling fans, air filters, and cabinet A/C.
  4. For overvoltage: review deceleration ramp; check for regen conditions and DB resistor if fitted.
  5. For comm loss: check EtherNet/IP path, switches, and PLC connectivity.
  6. Review drive parameters against last known-good backup.
Note: Drive fault information from Rockwell PowerFlex and similar drives is exposed via the Add-On Instruction; use the DriveFault sub-tag details on the PlantPAx faceplate for exact code.

5. Reset and Restart Criteria

  • Fault code cause identified.
  • Any electrical testing (megger, thermography) complete with acceptable results.
  • Drive cabinet cleaned / cooling verified if thermal fault.
  • Shift Supervisor approval logged.
  • Reset the drive fault, clear the MT-90100 alarm, and proceed with normal start sequence.
